MEE BRAND-WISE PRODUCT SEARCH UPDATE

Implemented in this source archive:
- Product Management search matches product name, brand, SKU and barcode.
- Product Management has an All Brands / individual Brand filter and a visible Brand column.
- Inventory search matches product name, brand and SKU; it has an individual Brand filter and visible Brand column.
- Inventory stock movement rows display the linked product brand.
- Billing product catalogue search accepts product name, brand, SKU and barcode; dropdown options show brand beside product name.
- Billing invoice line snapshots now retain brand and printed invoice item rows display it.
- Purchase product selectors show brand beside product name.
- Barcode list search matches brand and displays a dedicated Brand column.
- Internal Storefront product search matches brand and product cards display brand.
- Public storefront search and product cards display brand when supplied by the public store RPC.
- Reports include store brand names in the summary and CSV export.

No database migration is required: this project already has a products.brand field and product create/edit already saves it. Existing products without a brand will display an em dash until a brand is entered in Product Management > Edit Product.
